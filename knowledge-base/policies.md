# Store Policies

## Return & Refund Policy

### Eligibility
Products are eligible for return within 30 days of delivery, provided they meet the following conditions:
- Item is unused, unwashed, and in original condition
- All original packaging, tags, accessories, and manuals are intact
- The return request is raised through the app or website under "My Orders"

### Non-Returnable Items
The following categories are strictly non-returnable:
- Innerwear, lingerie, and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Customised or personalised products
- Digital gift cards and vouchers
- Products with broken seals (perfumes, skincare, supplements)
- Items explicitly marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable" on the product page
- Damage caused by improper use, accidents, or modifications

### Refund Timeline
After the returned item is picked up and quality-checked at our warehouse:
- UPI / Wallets: 1–2 business days
- Debit / Credit Cards: 5–7 business days
- Net Banking: 3–5 business days
- Cash on Delivery (refunded as store credit or bank transfer): 5–7 business days

### Defective or Wrong Item Received
If you receive a defective, damaged, or wrong product, raise a complaint within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will arrange a free pickup and send a replacement within 3–5 business days at no cost.

## Warranty Policy

### Electronics
All electronics carry the manufacturer's warranty:
- Headphones and earbuds: 1 year manufacturer warranty
- Power banks: 6 months manufacturer warranty
- Smart devices: 1 year manufacturer warranty

Warranty covers manufacturing defects and component failure. It does not cover physical damage, water damage, or damage from unauthorized repairs.

To claim warranty: Contact the brand's authorized service center with your purchase invoice. We provide a valid invoice by email for every purchase.

### Footwear
Footwear carries a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects (sole separation, stitching failure). Normal wear and tear, creasing, and colour fading are not covered.

### Bags & Accessories
Bags carry a 3-month warranty against zip failure and stitching defects. Material fading and scratches from regular use are not covered.

## Privacy Policy Summary

### Data We Collect
We collect your name, email, phone number, delivery address, and payment method (tokenised — we never store full card numbers). We also collect browsing and purchase behaviour to personalise your experience.

### How We Use Your Data
- To process and deliver your orders
- To send order updates via SMS and email
- To recommend products based on your browsing history
- To prevent fraud and ensure account security

### Data Sharing
We share your delivery address and phone number with our logistics partners (Delhivery, Shiprocket, BlueDart) solely for delivery purposes. We do not sell your personal data to third parties for advertising.

### Your Rights
You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by emailing privacy@example.com. We will process the request within 30 days.

## Shipping Policy

### Free Shipping
Free standard shipping is available on all orders above ₹499. Orders below ₹499 incur a flat ₹49 shipping fee.

### Express Delivery
Express 1–2 day delivery is available at an additional ₹99 for select pin codes in Tier 1 cities. Express delivery must be placed before 12:00 PM on a business day.

### Bulk & Business Orders
For bulk orders above ₹50,000, contact our B2B team at b2b@example.com for negotiated pricing, dedicated account management, and priority dispatch.

### Order Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before dispatch. Once dispatched, cancellation is not possible — use the return process after delivery.

## Seller & Marketplace Policy

All third-party sellers on our marketplace must:
- Be GST-registered businesses
- Provide only genuine products sourced from authorized channels
- Maintain a return acceptance rate above 90%
- Respond to customer queries within 24 hours

Sellers who fail to meet quality standards are suspended. Buyers are protected by our Buyer Guarantee — if a seller ships a counterfeit or wrong item, we will provide a full refund regardless of the seller's policy.
