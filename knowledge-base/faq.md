# Customer Frequently Asked Questions

## Ordering & Payments

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), debit cards, net banking, and EMI on orders above ₹3,000. Cash on Delivery (COD) is available on orders up to ₹10,000. We do not accept cheques or demand drafts.

**Q: Can I pay in EMI?**
A: Yes. EMI is available on orders above ₹3,000 through HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, and Kotak credit cards. EMI options of 3, 6, 9, and 12 months are available. No-cost EMI is available on select products for 3-month tenure.

**Q: Is it safe to enter my card details on your site?**
A: Yes. All payment pages are PCI-DSS compliant and use 256-bit SSL encryption. We never store full card numbers — only the last 4 digits for your reference.

**Q: My payment failed but money was deducted. What should I do?**
A: In most cases, failed payment deductions are automatically reversed within 3–5 business days by your bank. If you don't see the refund after 5 business days, contact our support at support@example.com with your bank transaction ID and we will escalate it.

## Delivery & Shipping

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
A: Standard delivery: 3–5 business days for metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune). 5–7 business days for other cities and towns. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available at ₹99 extra for select pin codes.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
A: Currently we ship only within India. We are working on international shipping and expect to launch by Q1 2025.

**Q: How can I track my order?**
A: Once your order ships, you'll receive an SMS and email with a tracking link. You can also track by entering your order ID at our website under "Track Order."

**Q: What if I'm not home during delivery?**
A: Our delivery partner will make 2 attempts. After 2 failed attempts, the package is held at the nearest delivery hub for 5 days. You can reschedule via the tracking link or contact our support team.

**Q: Do you deliver to Tier 3 cities and remote areas?**
A: We deliver to 24,000+ pin codes across India. Enter your pin code on the product page to check delivery availability and estimated date.

## Returns & Exchanges

**Q: What is your return policy?**
A: We offer a 30-day return policy for most products. Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags attached. Electronics must include all accessories and manuals. See our full Return Policy document for exclusions.

**Q: Can I exchange a product for a different size or color?**
A: Yes. Exchanges are free for footwear and clothing. For other products, a re-delivery fee of ₹49 applies. Initiate an exchange within 15 days of delivery through the "My Orders" page.

**Q: How long does a refund take after I return an item?**
A: Once we receive and inspect the returned item (2–3 business days after pickup), the refund is processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method. UPI refunds are fastest (1–2 days); card refunds may take up to 7 business days.

**Q: What items cannot be returned?**
A: The following cannot be returned: underwear and innerwear, personalized/customized products, perishable goods, digital downloads, and items damaged by misuse. Products bought during "Final Sale" events are also non-returnable.

## Products & Availability

**Q: How do I find the right shoe size?**
A: Use our Size Guide (available on each footwear product page). Stand on a piece of paper, trace your foot, and measure the longest point. Compare with our size chart. If between sizes, we recommend sizing up for running shoes and down for casual shoes.

**Q: Do you sell genuine/original products?**
A: Yes. All products sold on our platform are 100% genuine and sourced directly from authorized distributors or brand manufacturers. We have a zero-tolerance policy for counterfeit goods.

**Q: Can I request a product that isn't listed on your site?**
A: Yes, submit a product request via our "Request a Product" page. Our buying team reviews requests weekly and sources high-demand items. We'll notify you via email when the product becomes available.

**Q: Why is a product showing "Out of Stock"?**
A: We update inventory in real-time. Click "Notify Me" on the product page to get an email alert when it's back in stock. Most popular products restock within 7–14 days.

## Account & Loyalty

**Q: How does the loyalty rewards program work?**
A: You earn 1 point for every ₹10 spent. 100 points = ₹10 discount on your next purchase. Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity. Points cannot be used for COD orders.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your registered email and you'll receive a reset link within 2 minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes.

**Q: Can I have multiple delivery addresses saved?**
A: Yes. You can save up to 5 delivery addresses in your account under "Manage Addresses."
